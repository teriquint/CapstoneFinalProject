Platform Analysis

Instagram

Instagram is where schools feel the most visual and personal. I used this page to compare general university Instagram accounts with business school Instagram accounts to see whether they use the same platform in different ways.

General University Instagram

General university accounts usually show campus life, school spirit, student experience, community, events, and the overall personality of the school.

Business School Instagram

Business school accounts usually lean more toward careers, alumni success, admissions, networking, leadership, and hands-on learning.

Why this comparison matters

Looking at both account types helped me see how the same platform can tell two different stories depending on who the school is trying to reach.

General University Instagram Themes

This chart shows which themes appeared most often in the general university Instagram data. It helped me see how main university accounts use Instagram to build school personality, visibility, community, and connection.

Business School Instagram Themes

This chart shows which themes appeared most often in the business school Instagram data. It helped me see how business schools use Instagram to connect their message to programs, careers, alumni, admissions, and future opportunities.

Instagram takeaway

The Instagram comparison showed me that the same platform can do different jobs. General university accounts felt more focused on school identity, community, and belonging. Business school accounts were more likely to connect the school to careers, alumni success, admissions, and professional opportunities.
