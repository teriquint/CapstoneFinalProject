Platform Analysis

LinkedIn

This page looks at LinkedIn posts and comments from the schools in the sample. The charts show which themes appeared most often in the posts and how audiences responded in the comments.

LinkedIn Posts

LinkedIn posts included school updates, career-related content, alumni success, rankings, leadership, employer connections, and professional achievement.

LinkedIn Comments

LinkedIn comments showed audience responses to school content, including support, congratulations, alumni pride, questions, praise, and short public reactions.

A closer look at LinkedIn “other”

The LinkedIn “other” categories were different from the Instagram “other” category. In the LinkedIn data, “other” mostly included posts or comments that were broad, short, unclear, or did not fit neatly into one of the main codebook themes.

What the LinkedIn data shows

The LinkedIn data shows a more professional side of higher education marketing. The post themes included academic quality, rankings, alumni success, and school pride, while the comments were often short public responses such as praise, congratulations, questions, and school pride.

The closer look at “other” also showed that LinkedIn used that category differently from Instagram. On LinkedIn, “other” was more often tied to broad posts, short comments, unclear context, or quick reactions.
