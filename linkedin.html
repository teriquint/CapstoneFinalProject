Platform Analysis

LinkedIn

LinkedIn shows the professional side of higher education marketing. For this project, I looked at LinkedIn posts and comments to see how schools communicate credibility, alumni success, rankings, employer connections, and professional value.

LinkedIn Posts

LinkedIn posts show how schools present themselves in a professional space. These posts often highlight achievement, rankings, faculty expertise, alumni success, employer connections, and leadership.

LinkedIn Comments

LinkedIn comments show how audiences respond to school content. These responses can reveal alumni pride, support, congratulations, professional networking, or general engagement.

Why this comparison matters

Comparing posts and comments helps show both sides of LinkedIn branding: what schools choose to promote and how their audience reacts to that message.

LinkedIn Post Themes

This chart shows which themes appeared most often in the LinkedIn posts. These posts help show how schools use LinkedIn to build professional credibility and connect their brand to achievement, reputation, alumni, employers, and career value.

LinkedIn Comment Themes

This chart shows which themes appeared most often in the LinkedIn comments. The comments help show how audiences respond to higher education marketing in a professional platform, especially through support, praise, alumni pride, and brief public reactions.

LinkedIn takeaway

The LinkedIn data suggests that this platform works differently from Instagram. Instead of focusing mainly on visual identity or student life, LinkedIn is more focused on credibility, professional success, alumni visibility, rankings, and employer connections. The comments add another layer by showing how audiences often respond with brief, supportive, and achievement-focused reactions.
