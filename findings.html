Findings

This page is where the main story of the project can come together. The goal is to explain what the coded data reveals about how platform choice changes the way schools market themselves.

Finding 1

Instagram appears more emotional and identity-based, while LinkedIn appears more professional and credibility-based.

Finding 2

Business school accounts sit between university branding and career-focused marketing because they need to create both belonging and value.

Finding 3

LinkedIn comments often function as public affirmation, especially when audiences respond with praise, congratulations, alumni pride, or support.
