Platform Analysis

Business School Websites

Business school websites show the most official and controlled version of a school’s marketing message. Unlike Instagram and LinkedIn, websites are not built around a feed. They work more like a home base where schools organize their program identity, admissions information, rankings, curriculum, career outcomes, and return on investment.

Official Program Story

Websites present the school’s most polished version of itself, including program descriptions, degree options, rankings, curriculum, and admissions details.

Career and Value Messaging

Many business school websites emphasize career outcomes, employer connections, alumni success, leadership development, and the value of the degree.

Why this comparison matters

Comparing websites with Instagram and LinkedIn helps show the difference between official institutional messaging and more social, platform-based branding.

Website takeaway

The website comparison shows that business school websites function as the official “proof” layer of each school’s marketing. Instagram often builds emotion, visibility, and belonging, while LinkedIn highlights professional achievement and credibility. Business school websites provide the most complete program story by organizing the brand around outcomes, rankings, curriculum, career support, alumni networks, and return on investment.
