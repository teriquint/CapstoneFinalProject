Project Sources

Sources and Data Used

This page includes the data and outside sources that helped shape my project. I used Instagram posts, LinkedIn posts and comments, school websites, rankings, and background research to look at how business schools present themselves online.

Source Overview

I used a mix of collected data, rankings, websites, and research.

The biggest part of this project came from the data I collected myself. I looked at two years of Instagram posts and two years of LinkedIn posts from the schools in my sample when that information was available. For Instagram, I focused on visual messaging, post themes, likes, comments, and average engagement. For LinkedIn, I looked at posts, reactions, reposts, and comments.

The LinkedIn comments became more useful than I expected. At first, I was mainly thinking about the bigger engagement numbers, but the comments helped me see how people were responding in a professional space. Even short comments, like congratulations or school pride, helped show that people were publicly interacting with the school’s message.

I also reviewed each school’s website because the websites showed the more official side of the story. That is where schools explained the program, career outcomes, rankings, admissions details, flexibility, student support, alumni networks, and return on investment.

Ranking sources from U.S. News and Poets&Quants gave me another layer of context. I used them to compare prestige with engagement, especially in the ranking graph that showed higher ranking did not automatically mean higher Instagram engagement.

Primary data I collected

  • Two years of public Instagram posts from the selected schools when available.
  • Two years of public LinkedIn posts from the selected schools when available.
  • Public LinkedIn comments connected to the LinkedIn posts in my sample.
  • Engagement data, including likes, comments, reposts, and average engagement.
  • Official university, business school, MBA, and graduate program websites.
  • U.S. News and Poets&Quants ranking information used as context for comparison.
