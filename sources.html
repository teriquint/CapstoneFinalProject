Project Sources

Sources and References

This page lists the sources used for rankings, school websites, platform data, visuals, and tools used in this project.

Ranking Sources

Business school rankings

I used rankings as background context for the schools in the sample. Rankings were not the main focus of the project, but they helped show where each business school sits in the larger MBA market.

  • U.S. News & World Report. 2026 Best Business Schools rankings. View source
  • Poets&Quants. 2025-2026 MBA rankings. View source

School Websites

Business school websites reviewed

I reviewed each business school website to compare what information was easy to find, what message stood out most, and whether the website made the program feel valuable, welcoming, flexible, and career-focused.

  • Duke University, Fuqua School of Business
  • University of Notre Dame, Mendoza College of Business
  • Northwestern University, Kellogg School of Management
  • University of Texas at Austin, McCombs School of Business
  • University of Georgia, Terry College of Business
  • University of Michigan, Ross School of Business
  • Southern Methodist University, Cox School of Business
  • Baylor University, Hankamer School of Business
  • Vanderbilt University, Owen Graduate School of Management
  • Texas Christian University, Neeley School of Business

Platform Data

Instagram and LinkedIn data

I collected Instagram and LinkedIn posts from general university accounts and business school accounts. The data covered approximately the past two years and was coded using project codebooks and spreadsheet dropdown menus.

Tools and Visuals

Tools used in the project

  • Google Colab was used to analyze data and create charts.
  • Spreadsheets were used to organize posts, comments, codebooks, and coding categories.
  • HTML and CSS were used to build the final website.
  • Mapbox was used to create the school location map.

AI Use

How AI was used

I used AI tools to help brainstorm layout ideas, revise wording, troubleshoot HTML/CSS, and organize parts of the project. I reviewed and edited the final content myself, and the data coding, analysis decisions, and final interpretation were my own.
