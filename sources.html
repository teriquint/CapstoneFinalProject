Project Sources

Works Cited

This page includes the data, websites, rankings, screenshots, and outside sources that helped shape my project. I used these sources to look at how business schools present themselves across Instagram, LinkedIn, and official websites.

Source Overview

Data and source types used

The main part of this project came from data I collected myself. I looked at two years of Instagram posts and two years of LinkedIn posts from the schools in my sample when that information was available. I also reviewed LinkedIn comments, school websites, ranking sources, and public website screenshots.

  • Two years of public Instagram posts from the selected schools when available.
  • Two years of public LinkedIn posts from the selected schools when available.
  • Public LinkedIn comments connected to the LinkedIn posts used in my sample.
  • Engagement data, including likes, comments, reposts, and average engagement.
  • Official university, business school, MBA, and graduate program websites.
  • U.S. News and Poets&Quants ranking information used as context for comparison.
  • Public website screenshots and LinkedIn comment screenshots used as visual examples.
