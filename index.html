Higher Ed Marketing Across Platforms

How Universities Shape Their Story Online

This capstone explores how universities and business schools present themselves across Instagram, LinkedIn, and official websites. By comparing themes like student life, prestige, career outcomes, alumni success, and community belonging, this project looks at how schools adjust their message depending on the audience and platform.

Explore Instagram Explore LinkedIn View Findings

Research Question

Do schools tell different stories on different platforms?

This project compares how universities and business schools use platform-specific marketing. Instagram often emphasizes visibility, personality, student life, and belonging. LinkedIn often emphasizes professionalism, rankings, alumni success, employer relationships, and career value.

Instagram

Visual storytelling, school personality, student life, campus culture, events, and belonging.

LinkedIn

Professional credibility, alumni success, rankings, employer connections, and achievement.

Business Schools

Career outcomes, experiential learning, networking, admissions, and value of the degree.

Schools in the Study

The map shows the universities included in the sample: Baylor, Duke, Georgia, Michigan, Northwestern, Notre Dame, SMU, TCU, UT Austin, and Vanderbilt.

Data Sources

Four coded datasets

The analysis is based on coded data from general university Instagram accounts, business school Instagram accounts, LinkedIn posts, and LinkedIn comments. Each post or comment was categorized using a codebook so platform patterns could be compared more clearly.
