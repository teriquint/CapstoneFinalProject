Higher Ed Marketing Across Platforms

How Universities Shape Their Story Online

This capstone looks at how universities and business schools show up online. I wanted to see whether they tell the same story across Instagram, LinkedIn, and business school websites, or if the message changes depending on where people find them.

Explore Instagram Explore LinkedIn Explore Websites View Findings

Research Question

Do schools tell different stories across platforms?

This project looks at how universities and business schools tell their stories across Instagram, LinkedIn, and business school websites. Instagram often shows school personality, student life, and belonging. LinkedIn focuses more on professional success, rankings, alumni, and career value. Business school websites give the official program information, including curriculum, admissions, rankings, outcomes, and return on investment.

Project Focus

What I wanted to understand

I wanted to see how business schools present themselves online and whether the message changes depending on where you find them. Instagram feels more visual and personal, LinkedIn feels more professional, and websites are where schools give the full program story. This project looks at how all three work together to shape a school’s online presence.
