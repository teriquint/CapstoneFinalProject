Higher Ed Marketing Across Platforms

How Universities Shape Their Story Online

This capstone explores how universities and business schools present themselves across Instagram, LinkedIn, and business school websites. By comparing themes like student life, prestige, career outcomes, alumni success, and community belonging, this project looks at how schools adjust their message depending on the audience and platform.

Explore Instagram Explore LinkedIn Explore Websites View Findings

Research Question

Do schools tell different stories across platforms?

This project compares how universities and business schools use Instagram, LinkedIn, and business school websites to shape their public identity. Instagram often emphasizes visibility, personality, student life, and belonging. LinkedIn often emphasizes professionalism, rankings, alumni success, employer relationships, and career value. Business school websites provide the official program story through curriculum, admissions information, rankings, career outcomes, and return on investment.

Instagram

Instagram highlights visual storytelling, school personality, student life, campus culture, events, and belonging.

LinkedIn

LinkedIn emphasizes professional credibility, alumni success, rankings, employer connections, leadership, and achievement.

Business School Websites

Business school websites present the official program story through admissions messaging, curriculum, rankings, career outcomes, and degree value.

Data Sources

Four coded datasets

The analysis is based on coded data from general university Instagram accounts, business school Instagram accounts, LinkedIn posts, and LinkedIn comments. Each post or comment was categorized using a codebook so platform patterns could be compared more clearly.
